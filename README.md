# JennyCEO Hub

Build a High-Conversion Demo Website for JENNYCEO

ROLE

You are an expert product designer, conversion-focused web designer, UX strategist, and senior frontend developer.

I am creating a sales demo/prototype, not merely a generic business website.

The prospect is JennyCEO, a Nigerian importation/pre-order business that has grown primarily through WhatsApp, social media, YouTube, and community-based selling.

I previously reached out to the business owner suggesting that the business had reached a point where WhatsApp alone was becoming limiting.

She responded positively and explicitly agreed that:

WhatsApp reaching capacity is a real issue

Repeatedly answering the same customer questions is inefficient

New customers need a smoother way to understand the business

A website could become a central hub for the business

She intends to revisit the idea as part of the next phase of growth

Therefore, this demo must visually prove the idea I described to her.

Do NOT make this look like a generic template website.

The objective is for the business owner to look at the demo and immediately think:

"This is actually how my business should work."

BUSINESS CONTEXT

Brand

Business name:

JENNYCEO

Business model:

JENNYCEO operates a group-buying/importation pre-order model.

Customers pre-order products sourced from factories in China.

The company combines customer demand/resources to meet factory minimum order quantities and access factory-level pricing.

Products are shipped to Nigeria primarily by sea, with air shipping available at a higher cost.

Sea shipping

Typical duration:

2–3 months after leaving China

It may arrive sooner, but customers should understand that 3 months is the maximum stated expectation.

Air shipping

Typical duration:

7–14 days after the order leaves China

Air is faster but more expensive.

Important operational rule

When goods arrive in Nigeria, customers have 3 days to pay the applicable shipping balance for sea shipments.

Failure to pay can result in:

Daily demurrage/storage charges

Ultimately forfeiture/auctioning of the item to recover the company's costs

Business risk disclaimer

Customers must understand that the company cannot be held responsible for:

Delays in arrival

Wrong colours

Wrong sizes

Factory errors

Damage in transit

High or low shipping rates

Other risks associated with importation

The existing group policy explicitly states that remaining in the group constitutes acceptance of these rules.

CORE DEMO THESIS

The website should demonstrate that JENNYCEO can move from:

"Come into the WhatsApp group and figure everything out."

to:

"Here's one central place where customers can understand the business, see what is available, understand the process, get answers, and know exactly what to do next."

The website should therefore function as a customer education + product discovery + conversion hub.

Do not overcomplicate it with unnecessary SaaS features.

The demo should feel like a realistic first version of the business's future digital home.

PRIMARY USER

Design primarily for a Nigerian customer discovering JENNYCEO through:

YouTube

Instagram

Facebook

WhatsApp

Word of mouth

Existing community members

Assume the visitor knows little or nothing about pre-order importation.

The website must answer the visitor's questions before they need to send Jenny a DM.

PRIMARY CONVERSION

The primary conversion should be:

Explore Current Pre-Orders / Shop Pre-Orders

Secondary conversions:

Join WhatsApp Community

Ask a Question

Learn How Pre-Ordering Works

Explore Importation Course

Do not make "Contact Us" the only CTA.

The site should demonstrate that customers can become informed before contacting Jenny.

IMPORTANT UX PRINCIPLE

Every section should answer one of these questions:

What is JENNYCEO?

What can I buy here?

How does the pre-order system work?

How much does it cost?

How long does it take?

What happens after I order?

What risks/rules do I need to understand?

What happens when my goods arrive?

How do I place an order?

What if I want to learn importation myself?

The customer should not have to hunt for these answers.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE

Build the homepage as a highly polished conversion funnel.

SECTION 1 — HERO

Create a strong hero section.

Headline direction:

Import Smarter. Buy at Factory-Level Prices.

Pre-order products from China through JENNYCEO's group-buying system — with clear processes, shipping options and support from order to arrival.

Primary CTA:

Secondary CTA:

How It Works

Include a subtle trust statement such as:

China → Nigeria | Air & Sea Shipping | Pre-Order Model

Do NOT fabricate years in business, number of customers, revenue, student counts, testimonials, awards, certifications, or other social proof.

If real statistics are unavailable, don't invent them.

SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM

Make the process visually obvious.

Create a visually strong comparison.

SECTION 7 — "WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I ORDER?"

↓

↓

Include a highly visible note:

For sea shipments, customers have 3 days after arrival in Nigeria to pay the required shipping amount. Delayed payment may result in demurrage/storage charges and, ultimately, forfeiture of goods.

This should be easy to understand, not buried in tiny text.

SECTION 8 — CUSTOMER EDUCATION / FAQ

Create a large FAQ section.

Questions should include:

What does "pre-order" mean?

Explain that the items are not necessarily physically available in Nigeria when ordered.

Why does shipping take so long?

Explain the China → Nigeria sea shipping process.

Can I ship by air?

Yes. Air shipping is available and is faster but more expensive.

How long does sea shipping take?

Approximately 2–3 months after leaving China, subject to the realities and risks of international shipping.

How long does air shipping take?

Approximately 7–14 days after the order leaves China.

What happens if my goods arrive and I don't pay shipping?

Explain the 3-day payment requirement and possible demurrage/forfeiture consequences.

What if the factory sends the wrong colour or size?

Explain that factory errors are among the risks customers accept under the business policy.

What if shipping costs change?

Explain that shipping rates can vary.

Is my order guaranteed?

Do not make guarantees that the business has not stated.

The FAQ should dramatically reduce the number of repetitive questions Jenny has to answer manually.

SECTION 9 — TRUST / WHY JENNYCEO

Do not fabricate testimonials.

Instead, communicate the actual business model:

Why customers choose the JENNYCEO model

Factory-Level Buying

Customers participate in pooled orders that help meet factory minimum quantities.

Structured Pre-Orders

Customers know they are purchasing through a pre-order model rather than expecting immediate local stock.

Multiple Shipping Options

Choose between slower, lower-cost sea shipping and faster air shipping.

Guided Process

Customers have a defined journey from pre-order to arrival.

Keep this section credible and factual.
